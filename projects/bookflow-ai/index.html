PROJECT CASE STUDY / 002

BookFlow AI.

A booking and business-management platform for service-led businesses. BookFlow supports salons, nail technicians, barbers, designers and tailors, event planners, and any business that manages appointments, enquiries or customer bookings. It combines booking workflows with customer, staff and business tracking, while making WhatsApp a central channel for customer communication and booking.

  • Early accessPublic preview, active development
  • Multi-tenant SaaSTenant-scoped access and row-level security
  • Full stackNext.js, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL
  • Malawi firstDesigned with other African markets in mind

THE IDEA

Run the business,
not the diary.

Salons, nail technicians, barbershops, designers and tailors, event planners, photographers, trainers, consultants and other booking-led businesses often coordinate customers through WhatsApp, notebooks and disconnected tools. BookFlow brings scheduling, enquiries, customer records, staff and day-to-day business tracking into one workspace.

WhatsApp is a core part of the product direction: customers should be able to discover availability, ask questions and manage bookings through the channel they already use.

My role covers product concept, solution architecture and application development.

NOTE ON THE PREVIEW

The dashboard demonstrates the product direction while backend and third-party integrations are still being connected and tested. WhatsApp booking is a key planned integration and is not yet live. Google Calendar sync, payment processing and automated notifications also remain on the roadmap.

PRODUCT SCOPE

One workspace.
Different businesses.

Appointments & bookings

Manage service slots, customer requests, confirmations and the day’s schedule.

Customer & staff records

Keep customer details, service history and team information organised.

Business tracking

Give owners a clearer view of bookings and everyday business activity, not just a calendar.

WhatsApp-first interaction

Make it easier for customers to enquire, check availability and manage bookings through familiar conversations.
